    Compatibility, without guesswork.

    Bluetooth advertisements, USB volumes and mDNS services are discovery signals, not a universal control standard. Hooop supports direct BLE commands for Brother Skitch PP1 only. USB file copying works to selected mounted drives; direct LAN upload works only when you configure a compatible HTTPS receiver. Mobile phone access requires your running, paired local companion and trusted HTTPS. Cloud Hooop does not remotely control your desktop by itself.
